- **Web: the `webgl` feature compiles for `wasm32` without wgpu's `fragile-send-sync-non-atomic-wasm`.** The process-wide cache of wgpu instances was a `static` behind a `Mutex`, and with `webgl` wgpu's types are neither `Send` nor `Sync` on `wasm32` (44 errors, all at that one line). It is a `thread_local!` there, since the browser gives wasm one thread; native keeps the `static`. An app no longer needs to turn the feature on to build.
