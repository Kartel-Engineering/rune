- `Lens::prism()` takes `bend`, `split`, `shade` and `ring`, and its `light` scales the rim's two lights; each is 1 as fitted, so the default prism draws the same pixels. With `El::backdrop_blur` a prism is frosted: its middle shows the blur and its rim, bands, lights and ring stay sharp (before, a blur did not change it). `rune_kit::Glass` holds these settings, the frost, a tint alpha and the shadow; `rune_kit::glass_with(el, radius, &glass, tint)` draws them (`glass` is `glass_with` with `Glass::default()`), and `Glass::to_json` writes them as one flat JSON object.
