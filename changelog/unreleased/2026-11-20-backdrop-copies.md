- **`Cx::backdrop_copies()`: whether a window's own passes can copy its surface, which `El::backdrop_blur` and `El::backdrop_lens` need.** True on a GPU whose surface allows copies; false on the CPU painter, on a WebGL 2 canvas, after a device loss and in an embedded `LayerHost`, where both effects draw only the element's fill, so a view can keep that fill opaque enough to read over sharp content. True does not promise that every effect draws: past `MAX_BACKDROP_BLURS` or `MAX_BACKDROP_LENSES`, in a pass the app owns (`Renderer::draw_range` from a compositor) and in a frame with no pooled backdrop of the target's format, an effect still draws only its fill. `Runtime::set_backdrop_copies` sets it for tests.
