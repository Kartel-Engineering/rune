- **`Cx::backdrop_copies()`: whether `El::backdrop_blur` and `El::backdrop_lens` draw on this window.** True on a GPU whose surface can be copied from; false on the CPU painter, on a WebGL 2 canvas and after a device loss, where both effects draw only the element's fill, so a view can keep that fill opaque enough to read over sharp content. `Runtime::set_backdrop_copies` sets it for tests. `El::backdrop_lens` now refracts twice (into the glass and out of its flat bottom, `gap` above the backdrop), absorbs a little along the path so black stays black, draws premultiplied inside layers, clamps its sizes to 1000 points, counts as paint for the motion gate, and its pipeline is in `Renderer::first_use_ms`.
