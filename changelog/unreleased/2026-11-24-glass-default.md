- **The default look of `rune_kit::glass` changed.** `Glass::DEFAULT` (and so `glass(el, radius)`) is now a lightly bent, lightly frosted prism with a faint white veil and hairlines over a wide, soft shadow: `bend 0.19, split 0, shade 0.196, lights 0.821, ring 0.071, frost 1.714, tint 0, shadow_x 4.286, shadow_y 21.429, shadow_blur 61.429, shadow_spread 21.429, shadow_alpha 0.185, veil 0.052, hairline 0.167`. It was `Lens::prism()` at its fitted look under one close shadow: `bend 1, split 1, shade 1, lights 1, ring 1, frost 0, tint 0, shadow_x 3.28, shadow_y 9.82, shadow_blur 33, shadow_spread 0, shadow_alpha 0.118, veil 0, hairline 0`. For the old look, call `glass_with(el, radius, &Glass { bend: 1.0, split: 1.0, shade: 1.0, lights: 1.0, ring: 1.0, frost: 0.0, shadow_x: 3.28, shadow_y: 9.82, shadow_blur: 33.0, shadow_spread: 0.0, shadow_alpha: 0.118, veil: 0.0, hairline: 0.0, ..Glass::DEFAULT }, Color::TRANSPARENT)`. `Lens::prism()` itself is unchanged.
