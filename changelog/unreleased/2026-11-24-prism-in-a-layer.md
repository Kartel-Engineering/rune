- **`Lens::prism()` inside a see-through layer shows the layer, not black.** It read the layer's copy as opaque, so where the layer held nothing under the glass it drew opaque dark glass, and the layer's opacity faded that dark glass in over the window: a glass element under a fading ancestor (the default enter and exit transition) turned dark grey to black for the fade, darker as it went on, then snapped to its look. The prism now keeps the backdrop's alpha: where the layer is transparent the glass is too, and the window shows through. Over an opaque backdrop it draws the same pixels as before.
