An app that relied on the 9th to 16th lens falling back to its fill now gets glass there. Past the limit a lens or blur still draws only its fill, and now its outer shadows leave its middle clear, as they do where the effect draws: before, with no fill (a translucent fill already left the hole), the whole shadow showed through the clear element as a dark cloud.
