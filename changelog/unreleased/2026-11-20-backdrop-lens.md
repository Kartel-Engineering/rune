- **`El::backdrop_lens(Lens)`: glass that bends what is behind an element.** A clear slab rests on the backdrop, flat on top with a rounded shoulder: its middle shows the backdrop as it is, its shoulder pulls the backdrop in from beyond the rim, and the index of refraction varies with wavelength, so light and dark edges fringe in colour. `Lens` sets thickness, shoulder width, height above the backdrop, index, dispersion and the light its rim reflects. With `El::backdrop_blur` it is frosted glass: the flat top shows the blur and the shoulder fades from the sharp backdrop to it. It shares the backdrop blur's copy, has its own frame limit, `MAX_BACKDROP_LENSES` (8), and falls back as a blur does: past the limit, in a target that can't be copied, or on the CPU painter, the element draws its fill (and its blur, if it has one, on the CPU). Apps that don't call it draw the same pixels.
