- `rune_kit::Glass` gains `veil` (a white veil's alpha over the glass and its tint, which lifts a dark backdrop to a grey) and `hairline` (white lines one point inside the top and bottom edges); both are 0 by default, so `glass` draws the same pixels, and `to_json` writes them last. A frosted `Lens::prism()` starts its blur nearer the rim as `bend` falls, and at `bend(0.0)` the blur reaches the rim; at the fitted bend of 1 it draws the same pixels as before. Together they give a flat frosted pill: `Glass { bend: 0.0, split: 0.0, shade: 0.0, lights: 0.0, ring: 0.0, frost: 17.5, veil: 0.14, hairline: 0.2, ..Glass::DEFAULT }`.
