- `rune_kit::Glass` gains `veil` (a white veil's alpha over the glass and its tint, which lifts a dark backdrop to a grey) and `hairline` (white lines one point inside the top and bottom edges); both are 0 by default, so `glass` draws the same pixels, and `to_json` writes them last. They are two new pub fields, so a full `Glass { .. }` literal without `..Glass::DEFAULT` no longer builds. The veil over a tint is one fill mixed in the frame's blend space (sRGB encoding by default), so it paints as the two layers do. Where the glass falls back to its fill (past `MAX_BACKDROP_LENSES`, or a frame that can't be copied) the hairlines still draw. Below `bend(1.0)` a frosted `Lens::prism()` starts its blur nearer the rim as `bend` falls, and at `bend(0.0)` the blur reaches the rim; at the fitted bend of 1 and above it draws the same pixels as before. Together they give a flat frosted pill: `Glass { bend: 0.0, split: 0.0, shade: 0.0, lights: 0.0, ring: 0.0, frost: 17.5, veil: 0.14, hairline: 0.2, ..Glass::DEFAULT }`.
