- **Web: the accessibility mirror follows an animated layout change.** An element that glided to a new place kept its old rect in the mirror until the next commit, seconds later, because the mirror was rebuilt only when a commit, the viewport or a scroll changed, and the frames that move a glide commit nothing. The mirror now also rebuilds when the drawn rects move, at most every 150 ms as it does while the app scrolls, and once more when the motion stops. Every rebuild starts that limit, also one that makes the same items (a pressable the mirror hides can move for good). A page's horizontal viewport that the browser scrolls rebuilds nothing. Screen readers and browser tests read the element where it is drawn.
