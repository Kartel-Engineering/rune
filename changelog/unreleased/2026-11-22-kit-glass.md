- **`rune_kit::glass(el, radius)` and `rune_kit::glass_draws(cx)`: liquid glass as one call.** `glass` gives an element the radius, `Lens::prism()` and one close, soft shadow with no spread (drawn for a 41 point radius, scaled to this one), and no fill. `glass_draws` is true when the frame can be copied, a GPU draws it and reduced motion is off; call `glass` only then and give the element a solid fill otherwise.
